04 / PRODUCT DESIGN CASE STUDIES

How I approach a product problem.

I start with the problem, understand the user's needs, map the experience, explore possible solutions, and then refine the interface.

CASE STUDY 01 · STUDENT PROJECT

DevJourney

PROBLEM

Students often need a simple way to document learning progress, projects and achievements without making the process feel complicated.

PRODUCT IDEA

DevJourney explores a focused experience for documenting and presenting a student's learning journey.

EXPERIENCE

The concept brings learning progress, projects and personal development into a structured digital experience.

WHAT I LEARNED

CASE STUDY 02 · STUDENT PROJECT

PRODUCT IDEA

EXPERIENCE

CASE STUDY 03 · PERSONAL PRODUCT DESIGN CONCEPT

Internship Application Tracker

PERSONAL CONCEPT — NOT A DEPLOYED PRODUCT
PROBLEM

Students applying to multiple internships can lose track of application status, deadlines, follow-ups and next steps.

USER

College students actively applying for internships.

PROPOSED SOLUTION

A simple dashboard that organizes applications by status and highlights upcoming deadlines and actions.

KEY SCREENS

Dashboard · Application details · Application timeline · Deadline/follow-up view

WHAT I WOULD VALIDATE

I would test whether students can quickly understand their application status, find upcoming actions and update an application without unnecessary steps.

DESIGN PROCESS

A simple way of moving from question to direction.

01 UnderstandIdentify the user's problem.
02 ExploreMap the user flow and possible solutions.
03 DesignCreate wireframes and interface concepts.
04 RefineImprove clarity, hierarchy and usability.
05 BuildTranslate the final direction into a working digital experience where applicable.
